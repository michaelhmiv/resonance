# Resonance IA download checklist

This is the concrete follow-up to the download plan. It lists **exact Internet Archive items to grab first**, grouped into packs, with a separate **skip list** for works already present locally.

## Ground rules

- Download **one canonical edition per work family**.
- If a work already exists locally, **skip it** unless a materially better edition is needed.
- Treat omnibus items carefully: if they contain multiple works already in the corpus, do not re-download duplicates just because the IA item is convenient.
- Prefer the **highest-value, broadest-use** editions first.

## Already local — skip these work families

Do **not** re-download these unless you are intentionally upgrading the edition:

- Augustine — *Confessions*
- Thomas Aquinas — *Summa Theologica*
- Teresa of Ávila — *Interior Castle*
- John of the Cross — *Dark Night of the Soul*; *Spiritual Canticle*

## Suggested verification after each download

- [ ] Confirm item title matches the canonical work family
- [ ] Confirm year is pre-1928
- [ ] Confirm it is not already represented locally
- [ ] Record the work family in the manifest before adding more editions
- [ ] If the item is an omnibus, split or chunk it before adding new overlapping material

## Notes

- Some IA titles are **multivolume** or have alternate spellings; keep only one canonical edition unless the second is clearly better.
- The current local corpus is already enough for the following work families, so those are intentionally omitted here: *Confessions*, *Summa Theologica*, *Interior Castle*, *Dark Night of the Soul*, *Spiritual Canticle*, *Pilgrim’s Progress*, *Imitation of Christ*, *Pensées*, *Practice of the Presence of God*, *Revelations of Divine Love*, and the existing Church Fathers omnibus.
