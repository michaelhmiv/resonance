# Resonance synthetic corpus list (Gutenberg + CCEL + IA)

## Source comparison
| Source | Strengths | Weaknesses | Best use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gutenberg | cleaner editions, authoritative metadata, easy plain text | smaller catalog than IA | prefer when a work is on Gutenberg |
| CCEL / ANF-NPNF | best patristic coverage, especially Church Fathers series | HTML extraction, less tidy discovery | mine for fathers/series volumes |
| Internet Archive | biggest breadth, multi-work editions, lots of pre-1928 scans | noisy metadata, duplicate editions | fill gaps and get extra works per author |
