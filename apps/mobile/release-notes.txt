Resonance — Automatic On-Device Embedding for Added Files + Settings Toggle.

What's in this build:
• Automatic embedding for newly added files (on by default): books, documents, and pasted notes added to your Personal Library now embed automatically in the background without requiring you to tap "Embed"
• Reliable background queue: fixes race conditions where adding new books mid-embedding or finishing a batch import could leave books queued; automatically begins embedding as soon as the Voyage-4-Nano embedder finishes downloading and recovers paused/interrupted works
• New Settings → Personal Library toggle ("Auto-embed added files"): enabled by default, with an option to disable automatic embedding and embed manually on demand instead
• Cleaner Personal Library status: removes unnecessary "Embed now" prompts while auto-embedding is enabled and shows live background embedding progress
