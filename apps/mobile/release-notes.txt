Resonance — Word Tab UX Sweep, Performance Overhaul & Background Sync.

What's in this build:
• Exit any book effortlessly: compact reading header with ← Books / ← Library back button, Android hardware Back support, and active Word tab re-tap reset
• Flush bottom reading action bar: eliminates the gap above the bottom tab bar and fixes Highlight Mode overflow with a dedicated highlight strip (including highlight removal and Note guardrails)
• Major Word tab performance fixes: single batched O(1) cross-reference lookup per chapter, memoized verse rows, cached Bible/Library queries, and batched SQLite passage inserts
• Seamless tab switching & deep-linking: fixes stuck tab transitions, adds Patterns → Word verse deep-linking, scrollable chapter grid (Psalms 1–150, Isaiah, etc.), Old/New Testament filters, and Library section navigation
• True background downloading, importing & embedding: closing sync/import modals is instant and never stalls or cancels active work; queues persist and auto-resume across foreground/background transitions with a live status banner on the Word tab
