# Golden set v1 — embedding bake-off instrument (DRAFT for Michael's review)

Modern journal entries written the way people actually write today, each hand-matched
to the passages that genuinely echo it. The bake-off embeds these entries and checks
whether the expected echoes land in the top results. Michael reviews the *matches* —
the question per entry is: "are these the echoes a pastor would pick?"

Scoring per entry: recall@10 (any expected echo in top 10), recall@3 for the first-listed
(primary) echo. Hard negatives must NOT appear in the top 10.

---

### G01 — new-parent exhaustion
**Journal:** "Up since 3 with the baby again. I love her so much it hurts but I am running on fumes and I snapped at my wife over nothing this morning. I just need sleep and to not feel like I'm failing everyone."
**Expected echoes:**
- Psalm 127:2 — "in vain you rise up early and go late to rest... he gives to his beloved sleep" (WEB)
- Matthew 11:28-30 — "Come to me, all you who labor... I will give you rest"
- Brother Lawrence, *Practice of the Presence* — on God's presence in menial, repeated tasks
**Hard negative:** Psalm 127:3-5 (children as arrows — same psalm, wrong echo for *this* entry)

### G02 — money anxiety
**Journal:** "Checked the bank account at midnight again. Mortgage, daycare, the car needs brakes. I do the math over and over and it never changes. I know I'm supposed to trust God with this but honestly I'm just scared."

- Philippians 4:6-7 — "in nothing be anxious... the peace of God"

## Coverage notes for the bake-off
- Scripture appears in every entry's expected set (the Bible pack must never whiff entirely).
- Author echoes span: Augustine (4), Kempis (3), Brother Lawrence (2), John of the Cross (2),
  Teresa (1), Julian (1), Pascal (1), Ignatius (1), Herbert (1).
- 4 hard negatives included (G01, G06, G13, G21) — topical-but-wrong results are the failure
  mode this app cannot afford.
- v2 candidates (not in v1): Desert Fathers sayings as expected echoes (corpus just landed),
  Eckhart on detachment (G10 variant), voice-memo and photo variants of the same entries.
