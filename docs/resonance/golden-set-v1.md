# Golden set v1 — embedding bake-off instrument (DRAFT for Michael's review)

Modern journal entries written the way people actually write today, each hand-matched
to the passages that genuinely echo it. The bake-off embeds these entries and checks
whether the expected echoes land in the top results. Michael reviews the *matches* —
the question per entry is: "are these the echoes a pastor would pick?"

Scoring per entry: recall@10 (any expected echo in top 10), recall@3 for the first-listed
(primary) echo. Hard negatives must NOT appear in the top 10.

---

### G01 — new-parent exhaustion
**Journal:** "Up since 3 with the baby again. I love her so much it hurts but I am running on fumes and I snapped at my wife over nothing this morning. I just need sleep and to not feel like I'm failing everyone."
**Expected echoes:**
- Psalm 127:2 — "in vain you rise up early and go late to rest... he gives to his beloved sleep" (WEB)
- Matthew 11:28-30 — "Come to me, all you who labor... I will give you rest"
- Brother Lawrence, *Practice of the Presence* — on God's presence in menial, repeated tasks
**Hard negative:** Psalm 127:3-5 (children as arrows — same psalm, wrong echo for *this* entry)

### G02 — money anxiety
**Journal:** "Checked the bank account at midnight again. Mortgage, daycare, the car needs brakes. I do the math over and over and it never changes. I know I'm supposed to trust God with this but honestly I'm just scared."
**Expected echoes:**
- Matthew 6:25-34 — "do not be anxious about your life... your heavenly Father knows"
- Philippians 4:6-7 — "in nothing be anxious... the peace of God"
- Augustine, *Confessions* I — restless heart / God as the soul's rest

### G03 — grief, father's death anniversary
**Journal:** "A year today since Dad died. I keep reaching for my phone to call him about stupid stuff — a leak under the sink, the game Saturday. Then I remember. The missing him doesn't get smaller, it just gets quieter."
**Expected echoes:**
- John 11:33-36 — "Jesus wept" at Lazarus's tomb
- Psalm 42:3 — "my tears have been my food day and night"
- Augustine, *Confessions* IX — on the death of Monica, grief and weeping

### G04 — anger at a coworker
**Journal:** "He took credit for my work in the meeting again, right in front of the director. I sat there smiling while my face burned. I have rehearsed what I'll say to him about forty times tonight and none of it is Christian."
**Expected echoes:**
- James 1:19-20 — "slow to anger, for the anger of man does not produce the righteousness of God"
- Ephesians 4:26-27 — "be angry and do not sin; do not let the sun go down"
- Kempis, *Imitation of Christ* I.16 — bearing the faults of others patiently

### G05 — chronic illness / waiting on results
**Journal:** "Scan results come Thursday. Three more days of this. I've prayed every way I know how and the waiting room in my head never empties. Whatever it says, I need to not be alone in it."
**Expected echoes:**
- Psalm 27:14 — "Wait for the LORD; be strong, and let your heart take courage"
- Romans 8:18-25 — groaning and waiting with patience
- John of the Cross, *Dark Night* — God's hiddenness in the night (general theme)

### G06 — dry prayer life / feeling nothing
**Journal:** "Prayed this morning and it felt like talking to the ceiling. Read the same chapter twice and retained nothing. I don't doubt God's there, I just can't feel any of it lately and I miss how it used to feel."
**Expected echoes:**
- Psalm 13 — "How long, O LORD? Will you forget me forever?"
- Teresa of Avila, *Interior Castle* — on dryness in prayer / seasons of aridity
- John of the Cross, *Dark Night* I — the night of sense, feeling nothing in prayer
**Hard negative:** Psalm 95 (worship exhortation — topical on prayer, wrong for dryness)

### G07 — gratitude, ordinary day
**Journal:** "Nothing special happened today and that's kind of the miracle. Coffee on the porch, the baby laughed at the dog, work went fine. A year ago I would have traded anything for a day this boring. Thank you, God, for boring."
**Expected echoes:**
- Psalm 103:1-5 — "Bless the LORD, O my soul, and forget not all his benefits"
- 1 Thessalonians 5:16-18 — "give thanks in all circumstances"
- Brother Lawrence, *Practice* — gratitude in ordinary work

### G08 — temptation / old habit pulling
**Journal:** "Deleted the apps, told my accountability partner, did all the right things, and I still almost caved tonight just because I was bored and alone in the house. The wanting doesn't care about my systems."
**Expected echoes:**
- 1 Corinthians 10:13 — "God is faithful... he will provide the way of escape"
- Romans 7:15-24 — "I do not do what I want, but I do the very thing I hate"
- Augustine, *Confessions* VIII — the divided will, "give me chastity, but not yet"

### G09 — forgiving a parent
**Journal:** "Mom called. Thirty years of her rewriting history and I still pick up hoping it'll be different. I want to forgive her — I mean actually forgive, not just go numb — and I don't know how to want that and protect myself at the same time."
**Expected echoes:**
- Matthew 6:14-15 / the Lord's Prayer — "forgive us our debts, as we also have forgiven"
- Colossians 3:13 — "forgiving each other; as the Lord has forgiven you"
- Lewis-free zone: use *Imitation of Christ* III — on forgiving injuries (Kempis)

### G10 — job loss / identity
**Journal:** "Got laid off Friday. Twelve years and a cardboard box. Everyone keeps saying 'you're not your job' and I nod, but if I'm not the guy who provides and builds things, I honestly don't know who I am at the breakfast table."
**Expected echoes:**
- Job 1:21 — "The LORD gave, and the LORD has taken away; blessed be the name of the LORD"
- Matthew 6:19-21 — treasure in heaven, identity not in stored wealth
- Ignatius, *Spiritual Exercises* — indifference to honor, wealth, and status (Principle and Foundation)

### G11 — loneliness in a crowd / new city
**Journal:** "Six months in this city and I have forty acquaintances and no friends. Church lobby small talk, work lunches, then home to an empty apartment. I am surrounded by people and completely unknown."
**Expected echoes:**
- Psalm 68:6 — "God settles the solitary in a home"
- Genesis 2:18 — "it is not good that the man should be alone"
- Augustine, *Confessions* IV — friendship and the grief of losing a friend (on true friendship)

### G12 — joy / answered prayer
**Journal:** "She said yes. I keep saying it out loud in the truck like an idiot. After all the years of wondering if this part of life was just not for me — God, you didn't forget me after all."
**Expected echoes:**
- Psalm 126 — "those who sow in tears shall reap with shouts of joy... The LORD has done great things for us"
- Luke 1:46-55 — the Magnificat, God remembering his servant
- Hannah's song, 1 Samuel 2 — rejoicing after long waiting

### G13 — doubt / intellectual crisis
**Journal:** "Read something tonight that I can't un-read and it knocked a leg out from under everything. I'm not looking for a pat answer. I just need to know if faith can survive my actual questions or if it only works when I don't look too close."
**Expected echoes:**
- Mark 9:24 — "I believe; help my unbelief"
- Jude 22 — "have mercy on those who doubt"
- Pascal, *Pensées* — the wager / the heart has its reasons (on faith and reason)
**Hard negative:** Psalm 14:1 ("the fool says in his heart, there is no God" — condemns the doubter; wrong echo)

### G14 — caring for an aging parent
**Journal:** "Drove Mom to her third appointment this week. She asked me the same question four times in the car and I answered it gentle every time and then cried in the parking lot. This is the long goodbye and nobody trains you for it."
**Expected echoes:**
- John 19:26-27 — Jesus entrusting his mother from the cross ("behold, your mother")
- 1 Timothy 5:4-8 — caring for one's own household/parents as godliness
- Ruth 1:16-17 — loyalty that stays ("where you go I will go")

### G15 — success / pride check
**Journal:** "Got the promotion and the raise and caught myself mentally rehearsing how I'd tell people. Half of me is grateful and half of me is already afraid of looking smaller than this new title. Success is doing weird things to my heart."
**Expected echoes:**
- Deuteronomy 8:17-18 — "beware lest you say in your heart, 'My power... has gotten me this wealth'"
- Luke 12:16-21 — the rich fool building bigger barns
- Kempis, *Imitation* I.1-2 — humility, the vanity of worldly honor

### G16 — anxiety at night / insomnia
**Journal:** "3 a.m. and my brain is running the highlight reel of everything that could go wrong — the baby's cough, the presentation, the roof. The dark makes every fear sound reasonable."
**Expected echoes:**
- Psalm 4:8 — "in peace I will both lie down and sleep; for you alone, O LORD, make me dwell in safety"
- Psalm 91:5 — "you will not fear the terror of the night"
- Julian of Norwich, *Revelations* — "all shall be well, and all shall be well"

### G17 — marriage friction / feeling distant
**Journal:** "We're not fighting, we're just... roommates with a mortgage and a baby. Ships passing. I miss my wife and she's right there on the other side of the couch. I don't know how we got here or how to say it without it becoming a fight."
**Expected echoes:**
- Song of Solomon 2:15 — "catch the little foxes that spoil the vineyards" (small erosions of love)
- Ephesians 5:25-33 — husbands, love your wives as Christ loved the church
- 1 Peter 3:7 — live with your wife in an understanding way

### G18 — confession / secret sin shame
**Journal:** "Nobody knows. That's the worst part — I can lead a small group and quote the verses and there's this whole other ledger nobody sees. The shame isn't even about the thing anymore, it's about the hiding."
**Expected echoes:**
- Psalm 32 — "when I kept silent, my bones wasted away... I acknowledged my sin to you"
- 1 John 1:8-9 — "if we confess our sins, he is faithful and just to forgive"
- Augustine, *Confessions* II — confessing the pear theft / the anatomy of hidden sin

### G19 — waiting for a child / infertility
**Journal:** "Another baby shower invitation in the mail. I smiled and RSVP'd yes and then sat in the car for ten minutes. Three years of this. I'm happy for her. I'm happy for her. If I say it enough times maybe both things stay true."
**Expected echoes:**
- 1 Samuel 1 — Hannah's grief and prayer ("she was deeply distressed and prayed to the LORD and wept bitterly")
- Psalm 113:9 — "he gives the barren woman a home"
- Elizabeth/Luke 1 — God remembering after long barrenness

### G20 — simple wonder / creation
**Journal:** "Stood on the dock before sunrise and the water was glass and the sky did that thing it does. No prayer words came, just stood there. Some mornings creation says it better than I can."
**Expected echoes:**
- Psalm 19:1-4 — "the heavens declare the glory of God"
- Job 38-41 — God's speeches from the whirlwind on creation
- Gerard Manley Hopkins is NOT in the corpus (skip) — use Herbert, *The Temple* — poems on wonder and praise

### G21 — burnout in ministry/work
**Journal:** "I have nothing left to give anybody. Every need feels like an accusation. I used to do this work out of love and now I'm just performing it. If one more person says 'God never gives you more than you can handle' I might actually scream."
**Expected echoes:**
- 1 Kings 19 — Elijah under the broom tree: "It is enough; now, O LORD, take my life" — and God feeds him and lets him sleep
- Exodus 18 — Jethro to Moses: "the thing is too heavy for you; you are not able to do it alone"
- Mark 6:31 — "come away by yourselves... and rest a while"
**Hard negative:** 1 Corinthians 10:13 misapplied ("God won't give you more than you can handle" — the entry explicitly recoils from it)

### G22 — fear for a child's safety/future
**Journal:** "The news was on at the tire shop and I just stood there thinking about the world my daughter is growing into. I can't bubble-wrap her whole life. I have to hand her to God every single day and my hands shake doing it."
**Expected echoes:**
- Matthew 18:10 / 19:14 — children and their angels; "let the little children come to me"
- Psalm 121 — "the LORD is your keeper... he who keeps you will not slumber"
- Hannah dedicating Samuel, 1 Samuel 1:27-28 — entrusting a child to God

## Coverage notes for the bake-off
- Scripture appears in every entry's expected set (the Bible pack must never whiff entirely).
- Author echoes span: Augustine (4), Kempis (3), Brother Lawrence (2), John of the Cross (2),
  Teresa (1), Julian (1), Pascal (1), Ignatius (1), Herbert (1).
- 4 hard negatives included (G01, G06, G13, G21) — topical-but-wrong results are the failure
  mode this app cannot afford.
- v2 candidates (not in v1): Desert Fathers sayings as expected echoes (corpus just landed),
  Eckhart on detachment (G10 variant), voice-memo and photo variants of the same entries.
